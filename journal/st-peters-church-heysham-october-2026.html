St Peter's Church, Heysham

12th October 2026

Journal Posts: Previous, Next

This post is part of a series of posts about a few days in Morecambe

Series Posts:  First, Previous, Next, Last

During our trip to Morcambe, we visited St Peter’s Church in Heysham.

The church was open, so we had a good look around the inside. We then walked around the churchyard, where I took the first four photos on this page. As you can see from the photos, the weather was dark and cloudy. Too dark for non-flash photos inside.

Behind the church, there’s a low hill called ‘The Barrows’, with views out to sea. At the top of the hill, there’s an old ruined Chapel. The following photos were taken from the hill.
