My Wife's Eye Operations Are Complete

11th November 2026

Journal Posts: Previous, Next

Huddersfield Narrow Canal, July 2025

We are nearly halfway through November, and the weather outside is grim. It’s not raining; it’s just dark, with grey clouds and a little wind. So, today’s photo is a summer scene down on the canal.

Eye Operations

Optega operated on my wife’s eye back on the 14th of October; I wrote about this here. Today is four weeks after that operation; she has to go to Specsavers for a final check-up. So we are off to town. On the bus so I can have a few more bevvies than usual.

The operation was a success; both eyes are now done. It’s taken just under five months to have them both done. If she had gone with the NHS, she’s pretty sure she would still be waiting for the initial assessment. The NHS paid for the operation. As I said back in October, this is probably a good thing (as long as the NHS are not paying more for it); it frees up NHS resources for more serious health issues.

The effect on her vision is amazing. Her sight has got better, though she still needs two sets of glasses. The other day, we were in the Travellers Rest enjoying a drink. She said she could actually read the text on the pump clips without glasses, before it was just a blur. She sees colours better; she was appalled to see she had been using the wrong colour lipstick. She thought one of her jumpers was pink when it was actually red.

It’s improved her mood no end. She was talking to the optician about this; he said that lots of people found this, but no one knows why. Before, she didn’t want to go out much. She wants to go out more now, and more importantly for us, she wants to travel more.

All she needs now is her new glasses; next week.

Kings Head

While my wife was in Specsavers, I walked around town, killing time before meeting Polly at the King’s Head, by Huddersfield Station. I hoped to get there half an hour before her so I could read in a quiet corner for a bit. She got there at the same time; her appointment was quicker than she expected.

Our favourite pub in town is the Sportsman, but unfortunately it becomes a creche in the afternoon. Several women use it with their young children. Last August I nearly stood on a toddler, crawling around on the floor near the bar, unprotected. The last time we were in, the children were still walking and crawling around the floor; this time the mothers were walking around with them, drinks in hand. At least they were looking after them this time. Not good. We gave up going there in the afternoon.

The King’s Head is one of my favourite pubs (Good Beer Guide and excellent prices). My wife isn’t so keen, but she’s happy sitting there for a bit. Her wine is inexpensive (Railway Greenfield, please note).

This pub is very useful for a pint before or after a train journey. I’ve often had a quick pint before catching the train to Liverpool. It’s far better than the other pub at the station, the Head of Steam.

The Ephesus

We decided it’d be nice to have a meal before going home. The Ephesus is an excellent Turkish restaurant, opposite the station. We went there.

We had an excellent meal, accompanied by a bottle of red wine.

Because we were early, we had a starter and a main, for £19 each. With wine, the meal cost us £60.
