Rhos-On-Sea

21st June 2026

Journal Posts: Previous, Next

This post is part of a series of posts about a weekend in Llandudno.

Series Posts:  First, Previous, Next, Last

All the pictures on this page are of the seafront at Rhos-On-Sea. There’s not much here, but the beach has one thing that Llandudno beach has very little of: sand. There are also a few interesting shops. For me, there is only one reason to come here: to have some bevvies in the Tapps@Rhos micropub.
