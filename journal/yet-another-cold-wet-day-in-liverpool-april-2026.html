Yet Another Cold Wet Day in Liverpool

14th April 2026

Previous, Next

Clouds Gathering over North Wales and the River Dee

Today was another cold and wet day. I’ve not had much luck with the weather this trip.

Did very little today except to get wet inside. I met to meet John for our last session of the trip, in the Ship and Mitre. Before that I had a couple of pints in another one of my favourite Liverpool pubs, the Baltic Fleet. Apart from sitting with mum and watching the quizzes on TV, that’s all I got up to today.

I somehow managed to get to the Baltic, and from there to the Ship without getting too wet.
