Llandudno Seafront, Early Morning

21st June 2026

This post is part of a series of posts about a weekend in Llandudno.

Series Posts:  First, Previous, Next, Last

Grand Hotel and the Pier

I was down at the seafront by seven this morning. There was hardly anyone around to get in the way of my photos. The sun was also above the bay, unlike yesterday’s pictures when it was behind the town.

Seafront Properties Looking Towards the Little Orme

Seafront Properties Looking Towards the Great Orme

By lunchtime, there will be hundreds of people walking around in this photo; now, there is hardly anyone.

Beach and Seafront Properties Looking Towards the Little Orme

The War Memorial

Taking the above photo with no one in it would be impossible by nine in the morning. Sometimes, you need to get up early to get a photo. Also, the light is better early in the day, much better than at lunchtime when it is at its highest and brightest.
