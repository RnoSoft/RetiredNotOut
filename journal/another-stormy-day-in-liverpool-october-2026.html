Another Stormy Day in Liverpool

26th October 2026

Journal Posts: Previous, Next

This post is part of a series of posts about a week in Liverpool, keeping Mum company

Series Posts:  First, Previous, Next, Last

Maltese Cruise Ship at the Pier Head

Just for a change, today was wet and windy, the tail end of the storm.

I didn’t get up to much: lunch at Mr Chilli and a session with John in the Baltic Fleet.

I originally planned to eat at Mowgli, as long as there were no screaming kids. On the way there, I changed my mind and decided to eat at Mr Chilli again. I really enjoyed Saturday’s meal. So much, I decided to have it again.

Then to the Baltic Fleet for a session with John. I’ve written about this excellent Good Beer Guide pub before.

The pub is difficult to get to and from, especially from John’s flat and Alison’s house. We decided to risk the 204 to get home. An infrequent bus that passes both residences. Luckily, we made it to the bus stop in time.

Back at Alison’s, it was the dreaded quizzes for a change.
