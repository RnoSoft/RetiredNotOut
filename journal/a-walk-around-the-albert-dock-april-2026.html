A Walk Around the Albert Dock

12th April 2026

Journal Posts: Previous, Next

This post is part of a series of posts about a trip to Liverpool in April 2026, to keep my mother company while my sister went on holiday.

Series Posts:  First, Previous, Next, Last

The photos on this page were taken during a walk around the inside of the Albert Dock this morning. As you can see, it was dark and gloomy with some rain, the sun wasn’t shining. Inside, you are undercover so at least I stayed dry. There was also a bit of a wind, as you can see from the ripples on the water.

When I walked round it was fairly quiet, but it can get busy. There are shops that mainly sell food and souvenirs. There are bars and restaurants.

Back in November I took several night photos at the Albert Dock, you can see them here and here.
