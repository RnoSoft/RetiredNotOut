Posts About My Trips Away from Home

Each of my trips away from home results in several posts on my journal. Together, they describe the trip, why I went on it, how I travelled, where I stayed and what I got up to. Together, they are a diary of it.

The first post is always tagged with this tag, other posts about the trip are not tagged with it. So, listed below are all my trips away since starting the tag. Clicking read more displays the first post for a given trip. Successively clicking next allows you to read all the posts about it.

A Week in Liverpool Keeping Mum Company

Today my sister went on holiday for a week in the sun, so I’m over in Liverpool keeping my mum company while she’s away. I’ll also be doing some photography and meeting my friend John for bevvies in some of our favourite Merseyside hostelries. I’m travelled there by train.
