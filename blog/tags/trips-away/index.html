Posts About my Trips Away from Home

Each of my trips away from home results in several posts on my journal. About the places visited, the things I got up to, the photographs I took and the pubs I visited. The first post for a trip is always about the trip itself and is tagged with this tag.

Listed below are my trips away from home since starting the tag. Selecting one of them takes you to the first post of the trip. Once there you can use next post to read about what I got up to.

A Week in Liverpool Keeping Mum Company

Today my sister went on holiday for a week in the sun, so I’m over in Liverpool keeping my mum company while she’s away. I’ll also be doing some photography and meeting my friend John for bevvies in some of our favourite Merseyside hostelries. I’m travelled there by train.
