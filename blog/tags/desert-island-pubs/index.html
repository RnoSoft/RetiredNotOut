Desert-Island-Pubs

I stole this idea from the BBC. They have a program called Desert Island Discs, in which guests choose their eight favourite songs. Just before they are cast away on the island, they must choose their favourite song to take with them.

Sitting in a pub one day, I thought it would be a much better idea to have pubs rather than songs. Then, just before being cast away, I’d have to choose my favourite to take with me to the island. Much, much better than taking a record. Because I no longer travel abroad, all pubs must be in Great Britain.

This tag is a work in progress; I’m still choosing my eight pubs.

Gregson Centre, Lancaster
